How I Rebuilt SEO for a Payroll Compliance Firm: The SNGSPL Case Study

By Duke Chadha · SEO & Performance Marketing Consultant · Delhi NCR

SNGSPL is a Gurgaon-based payroll and statutory compliance firm — the kind of business that lives or dies by trust signals in search. When HR and finance teams look for a compliance partner, they're not browsing casually; they're searching with intent, often under deadline pressure around filings, audits, or a labour law change. That made this engagement less about chasing traffic volume and more about owning the specific, high-intent queries this audience actually types.

Here's how the SEO strategy came together, end to end.

Starting with the technical foundation

Before touching content or keywords, I ran a full technical audit. SNGSPL's site had been through a migration, which meant the first job was untangling old-vs-new URL structures and cleaning up redirect chains that were quietly leaking link equity. I mapped every legacy URL, fixed broken redirects, and made sure Google Search Console wasn't flagging orphaned or duplicate pages.

Core Web Vitals were the next problem — specifically CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift) and LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) issues on mobile, which were dragging down both user experience and ranking potential. I traced these to unoptimized image loading and layout-shifting ad/embed elements, then worked through fixes page by page using Yoast and RankMath for on-page tracking alongside GSC's Core Web Vitals report.

Backlink audit and disavow strategy

Compliance-sector sites often accumulate low-quality, spammy backlinks over time — directory spam, scraped content, irrelevant foreign-language domains. I ran a full backlink audit covering roughly 500 referring domains, separated the toxic links from the legitimate ones, and built a disavow file to submit to Google. This is unglamorous work, but for a compliance brand, domain trust is everything — a spammy backlink profile actively undermines the credibility signals Google (and prospective clients) are looking for.

"For a compliance brand, the backlink profile isn't just an SEO metric — it's a trust signal that compounds or erodes over time."

Content strategy built around real search intent

Rather than generic blog filler, content was mapped directly to the regulatory calendar and the actual questions SNGSPL's audience searches for:

  • Labour Code updates — breaking down what changed and what employers need to do
  • TDS and Income Tax Act (2026) changes — payroll-specific implications, not generic tax content
  • Payroll compliance penalties — the searches people run when they're worried they've already made a mistake
  • Labour Welfare Fund — a dedicated post clarifying a topic with surprisingly thin competing content

Each piece was structured around pillar-cluster logic — building topical authority around "payroll compliance" as a whole, rather than isolated one-off posts competing with each other.

Keyword strategy and ongoing GSC analysis

I track performance directly against Google Search Console data — impressions, click-through rate, and average position by query — to see which content is gaining traction and which needs another pass. This isn't a one-time audit; it's an ongoing cycle: publish, monitor in GSC, refine titles and meta descriptions where CTR is underperforming impressions, and expand content clusters where I see rising impression volume on adjacent queries.

The core lesson for any compliance or B2B service business: SEO isn't just about ranking for volume keywords, it's about being the credible, technically sound answer when someone searches with real urgency.
